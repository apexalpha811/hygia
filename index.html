FOUR-ZONE COLOR-CODED MICROFIBER ISOLATION SYSTEM

To guarantee absolute biological containment, cloths and mops used in sanitary waste zones NEVER enter workstations or dining zones.

Zone Red
High-Risk Restroom Fixtures

Strictly designated for toilets, urinals, floor drains, and sanitary disposal fixtures. Stored in dedicated containment bins.

Zone Yellow
Restroom Counters & Walls

Used exclusively on restroom vanities, faucets, paper towel dispensers, soap fixtures, mirrors, and stall partitions.

Zone Blue
Workstations & Desks

Dedicated to executive desks, keyboards, office cubicles, conference tables, telephones, and general office furniture.

Zone Green
Breakrooms & Food Prep

Strictly reserved for kitchenettes, cafeteria counters, refrigerators, microwave exteriors, and eating surfaces.
